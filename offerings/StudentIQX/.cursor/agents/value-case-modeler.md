## Domain Knowledge
Higher-ed value levers (`HYPOTHESIS`, benchmark via research):
- **Retention/persistence:** each retained student ≈ retained net tuition +
  fees for remaining terms; a 1-point persistence lift across a cohort is large.
- **Enrollment yield/melt:** reducing summer melt / lifting yield → net tuition
  revenue; discount-rate optimization.
- **Advancement:** donor-propensity lift → incremental giving / larger gifts.
- **Cost-to-serve / staff efficiency:** less manual reconciliation and reporting;
  advisor caseload effectiveness.
- **Risk avoidance:** FERPA/compliance incident cost.
Build conservative/base/aggressive scenarios; keep claims **CFO-safe** and tie
each to `EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md` proof points. Net tuition (not sticker) is the right
basis. Ground every benchmark with a `VERIFY:` flag.
