## Domain Knowledge
Anchor regime (`HYPOTHESIS` until sourced): **FERPA** governs education records —
directory vs non-directory info, legitimate educational interest, consent,
disclosure, and the "school official" exception (relevant to Verndale as a
vendor/processor). Adjacent regimes to check (`VERIFY`): **GLBA Safeguards**
(financial-aid data), **state privacy laws** (e.g., CCPA/CPRA for applicable
data), **Title IV** program integrity, **HIPAA** where campus health data is
involved, **PPRA**, accreditation data requirements, and international students
(GDPR). AI-specific: explainability/consent for predictive risk scoring, bias/
fairness in retention/admissions models, and audit/logging obligations. Map each
constraint to Snowflake GOVERNANCE (consent records, PII classification, audit)
and Sigma access controls per the doctrine.

## Instructions
1. Read `AGENTS.md` and the two dependency outputs.

3. Emit `VERIFY:` flags for any current-law/enforcement claim; add

4. Make output machine-consumable (a YAML/table block of constraints) so Agents
