## Domain Knowledge
Higher-ed buying/budget realities (`HYPOTHESIS`): tight budgets, fiscal-year and
grant cycles, committee approval, thresholds (e.g., FERPA/data-governance
co-buyer required above a spend level per SRC-2). Tier ladder options (per
Blueprint): diagnostic/assessment (e.g., Student Data Discovery), prototype/
pilot, implementation accelerator, managed optimization, full platform build.
Map tiers to ICP segments and functional entry points (enrollment lead motion;
student success longest cycle; advancement parallel track). Snowflake/Sigma
licensing is the customer's (position Verndale services on top). Keep pricing
defensible against the unproven ICP — offer a low-risk entry (diagnostic) given
relationship thinness.

3. Label assumptions; keep pricing ranges honest and evidence-anchored.
