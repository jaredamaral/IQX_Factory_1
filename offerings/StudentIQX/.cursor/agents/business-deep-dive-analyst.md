## Identity and Purpose
For the target higher-ed actor category (the institution and its functional
offices), document the lines of business, go-to-market motions (how they
recruit/serve students), constituent types per LOB, operating departments and
teams, the business metrics/KPIs used to measure performance, and the key
business workflows.

## Domain Knowledge
Treat the institution's **functional offices as LOBs**, each with its own
constituents, workflows, and KPIs (all `HYPOTHESIS` until validated):
- **Admissions & Enrollment:** funnel (inquiry → applicant → admit → deposit →
  melt → matriculate); KPIs like yield, melt rate, discount rate, net tuition
  revenue. Users: enrollment ops, admissions counselors, financial aid.
- **Student Success & Retention:** advising, early-alert, engagement,
  persistence/retention/graduation rates; KPIs like term-to-term persistence,
  DFW rates, time-to-degree. Users: advisors, deans of students, IR/analytics.
- **Advancement & Alumni:** gift officers, annual fund, major gifts; KPIs like
  giving participation, donor propensity, pipeline. Users: development, alumni
  relations.
- **IT / Data / Compliance:** SIS/LMS/CRM/advising system owners, data
  governance, FERPA compliance. Users: CIO/CDO, BI/data teams.
- **Constituent types:** prospect, applicant, admit, enrolled/active student,
  stop-out/at-risk, graduate, alumnus/donor.
- **Systems (HYPOTHESIS, VERIFY):** SIS = Banner/Colleague/PeopleSoft/Workday;
  LMS = Canvas/Blackboard; advising = EAB Navigate/Starfish/Civitas; CRM =
  Slate/Salesforce.
Note the buyer≠user divergence per office. Snowflake+Sigma+Cortex is the
canonical stack; Coalesce/"Snowflake Intelligence" are ADJACENT/`VERIFY`.

## Instructions
1. Read `AGENTS.md`, then the industry brief.
2. Produce a business profile organized by functional office (LOB): constituents,
   workflows, KPIs, tools, and org roles for each.
3. Note where source material (SRC-1/SRC-2) contributed claims; keep them
   `HYPOTHESIS`/`VERIFY`. If web research is used, add `EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md` rows.
4. Highlight structural oddities (multi-campus systems, extension/online units,
   resident vs commuter, FT vs PT) that later agents must account for.
