## Headline Recommendation

**Recommendation: Revise.**

`HYPOTHESIS`: StudentIQX should stay alive, but Stage 1 should not receive a clean Proceed yet. The best current wedge is **Enrollment Melt + First-Term Handoff Intelligence**, packaged as a governed Snowflake/Sigma accelerator that scores deposited students, routes staff-owned interventions, drafts human-reviewed outreach content, pushes approved payloads to CRM/marketing tools, and ingests matriculation/first-term outcomes for correlation-only measurement.

The market pain is real enough to continue: enrollment volatility, demographic pressure, retention/persistence gaps, and data/AI governance needs are supported by Stage 1 evidence (`VERIFY(E-014/E-016/E-047/E-049/E-050/E-051)`). The commercial blocker is differentiation, not pain existence. `GATING VERIFY(E-058)`: EAB now explicitly markets a deposit-day-to-day-one melt workflow with incomplete milestone tracking, scaled personalized outreach, embedded AI agents, and net-tuition framing. Agent 8 therefore cannot recommend a broad go-to-market until StudentIQX proves why a governed Snowflake/Sigma decide/draft/track/ingest layer is valuable alongside or instead of EAB/Navigate360, Slate, Salesforce, Civitas, Ellucian, or internal BI (`VERIFY(E-052/E-054/E-055/E-056/E-057/E-058)`).

**Gate 1 decision option:** revise the wedge into a narrow validation package before Stage 2 proceeds at full depth. The revision is small and specific: validate the EAB comparison, budget holder, data access, and first-customer path for the melt/first-term handoff wedge.

## Top 3 Buyer Pains

1. `HYPOTHESIS`: Enrollment leaders cannot reliably see which deposited students are most likely to melt, why they are at risk, who owns the next action, what status the action is in, and whether the student matriculated.

   Business consequence: late melt response, weaker class-size and net-tuition forecast confidence, manual list work, and disputed yield/melt reporting (`VERIFY(E-031/E-058)`).

2. `HYPOTHESIS`: Student Success and advising inherit first-term students without enough pre-enrollment context, including aid friction, orientation/housing/payment status, prior outreach, and melt-risk history.

   Business consequence: first-term support starts after known risks have already materialized; early persistence and retention remain measurable pressure points (`VERIFY(E-049)`).

3. `HYPOTHESIS`: CFO, Finance, IR, Enrollment, and Student Success lack a trusted closed loop from score or segment -> staff owner -> action status -> outcome -> agreed metric definition.

   Business consequence: leaders cannot credibly decide whether intervention capacity, technology spend, or staff effort is protecting net tuition, improving persistence, or just creating activity (`VERIFY(E-029/E-031/E-053)`).

## Minimum Viable IQX Wedge

**Package name:** `HYPOTHESIS`: Melt-to-Matriculation Activation Accelerator.

**Scope:** deposited-student melt risk through first-term handoff.

**Minimum workflow:**

1. Ingest CRM/application/deposit, aid, orientation/housing/payment/registration, SIS, and contact-intelligence feeds.
2. Resolve identity from applicant/deposit record to SIS/matriculation state with match confidence.
3. Define governed yield, melt, matriculation, and census metrics with Enrollment, Finance, Registrar, and IR.
4. Create risk/cohort lists with reason codes.
5. Assign deposited students to staff owners by risk reason and office.
6. Use Cortex to draft email copy or call talktracks when appropriate.
7. Require human approve/edit before payload export.
8. Push approved payload to CRM/marketing; do not send email or place calls in StudentIQX.
9. Track decision, draft, approval, handoff, status, and external execution reference.
10. Ingest matriculation and first-term outcome feeds for correlation-only outcome views.

**Out of scope for MVP:** student-facing self-service, autonomous outreach, peer-to-peer activation as native workflow, broad advising replacement, advancement expansion, causal claims, and full bidirectional CRM payload completeness until Agent 13.
