# StudentIQX BDR Field Guide
### Who to call, what to say, what to ask

---

## The 30-Second Pitch

Higher ed institutions have student data scattered across a dozen systems: CRM, SIS, financial aid, housing, LMS, advising. Nobody can see the full picture of a single student, and that costs real money and real students — melt after deposit, first-year students who fall through the cracks, retention risk nobody catches in time.

**StudentIQX** is a governed intelligence and activation layer — built on Snowflake and Sigma — that sits *alongside* the systems they already have (not replacing them). It scores risk, tells staff who to act on and why, drafts the outreach with AI (human-approved before it ever goes out), and closes the loop by tracking what happened.

**Lead wedge:** Melt-to-Matriculation — catching deposited students at risk of not showing up, before it's too late. (Other wedges — retention, transfer, advancement — exist as future inventory. Don't pitch the whole platform; pitch this one.)

|---|---|
| "StudentIQX replaces Slate, Salesforce, EAB, Civitas, Starfish, SIS, or LMS." | "StudentIQX complements your existing systems." |
| "StudentIQX sends the emails / makes the calls." | "Staff decide, AI can draft, a human approves, your CRM executes." |
| Any ROI number, or "proven to improve retention by X%." | "We're building the first proof points together — that's what discovery is for." |
| "We already know who owns budget at your school." | "We're figuring that out together — who typically owns this kind of decision for you?" |
| "This is a broad Student 360 platform." | "The first thing we'd build with you is melt-to-matriculation." |

## Persona Playbook

| Role | Their Pain | Your Angle | Ask This |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VP Enrollment / Admissions** (primary buyer) | Can't see which deposited students are melting, or why, until it's too late | Governed melt-risk queue with reasons, owners, status, and outcomes | "How do you currently spot a deposited student going quiet before day one?" |
| **Director of Admissions / Enrollment Ops** | Rebuilding funnel/melt lists manually across CRM, aid, SIS every week | Turns fragmented signals into assignable queues with owner, status, outcome | "How much of your week goes into rebuilding lists that already exist somewhere else?" |
| **Admissions Counselor** | Outreach lists with no context on who's at risk or why | AI-drafted, reason-specific outreach ready for review, not a blank list | "When you call a deposited student, what do you wish you knew going in?" |
| **Financial Aid Officer/Director** | Aid friction invisible to whoever's doing outreach | Aid-gap flagged as a reason code, routed without over-exposing sensitive data | "Does an enrollment counselor see aid status when deciding who to call?" |
| **VP Student Success / Dean / Provost** | First-year students arrive with zero pre-enrollment context | Matriculation handoff carrying recruitment/risk history into advising | "What do your advisors know about a student before they ever meet them?" |
| **Academic Advisor** | Inherits students blind, no idea who's already flagged at-risk | Early-alert queue with reason, owner, status — not just a name | "How do you find out a student is struggling, and how early?" |
| **Retention Coordinator** | Risk signals in five systems, no shared owner or outcome log | One governed queue: risk type, owner, due date, outcome, auditable | "If two people reach out to the same at-risk student, would you know?" |
| **Registrar / Transfer Lead** | Transfer credit evaluation creates invisible delays | Assigned resolution workflow tracked to first-term success | "How long until a transfer student gets a clear credit-applicability answer?" |
| **CFO / VP Finance** | Can't tell if retention spend is protecting net tuition revenue | Closed loop: score → action → outcome, tied to revenue-relevant metrics | "How do you currently measure whether an outreach investment is working?" |
| **CIO / CDO / VP IT** (required co-buyer) | Owns FERPA risk for anything touching student data | Governed, auditable, role-based access — built for sign-off, not around it | "What's your approval process for a new tool touching student records?" |
| **Institutional Research Lead** | Operational and official metrics constantly disagree | One governed semantic layer per definition, without forcing offices to agree | "When Enrollment and IR report different retention numbers, then what?" |
| **VP Advancement / CAO** | Student history disappears the moment someone becomes an alum | Governed identity carried from student to alum | "How much on-campus history follows a student into your alumni database?" |
| **Gift Officer** | Propensity signals scattered, hard to act on | Assigned cultivation actions, correlational (not causal) outcome tracking | "How do you decide who to prioritize for outreach this quarter?" |
| **Alumni Relations** | Engagement data disconnected from giving data | Unified alumni engagement feeding both relationship and giving workflows | "Do engagement and giving teams work off the same data?" |
| **Data Engineer / Steward** | Constant one-off extracts and dashboard firefighting | Governed layer with clear source-of-truth boundaries | "What eats more of your time — new requests, or fixing old ones?" |

*Lead with the top 3-4 rows for cold outreach — they're closest to budget. Use the rest once you're in a conversation and know who else is in the room.*

## Probe Their Stack (once you know what they use)

Don't attack the tool. Learn where it stops.

- **Slate:** "Do counselors have one trusted view of aid, housing, and outreach history before day one? What happens when a risk signal belongs to Financial Aid instead of Admissions?"
- **Salesforce (Education/Marketing Cloud):** "Are journey results connected back to matriculation in a way Enrollment and Finance both trust?"
- **EAB/Navigate360, Starfish, Civitas:** "Does the platform cover melt and first-term handoff, or mostly kick in after matriculation? Can you see who owns the action and what happened?"
- **SIS (Ellucian/Workday/Banner):** "Where do teams still export to spreadsheets to build an action list?"
- **BI dashboards / warehouse:** "Do the dashboards tell staff what to do next, or just explain what already happened?"

**Fast personalization signals** to look for before you write: public enrollment pressure or discount-rate news, a recent CRM/SIS implementation (or a stalled one), heavy use of any tool above, strategic-plan language about retention or AI, or a transfer-heavy / first-year-success initiative.

## Email Opener to Adapt

> Many enrollment teams can see deposits, aid status, orientation, and outreach activity — but not in one staff-owned melt workflow. StudentIQX is built to show which deposited students need action now, why, who owns the follow-up, and whether they matriculated. It's designed to complement what you already run (Slate, Salesforce, SIS, advising) — not replace it. Worth 15 minutes to compare notes on where melt-risk signals break down today?

## Verndale Proof Points You Can Use

- Deep analytics and data experience unifying fragmented data into decision-ready views.
- Strong customer experience expertise — this is about better next actions, not just better reports.
- Real investment in higher-ed domain knowledge: enrollment, retention, student success, governance, the systems already common in this market.

**Don't say:** "We've already proven this exact ROI in higher ed." We haven't yet — that's what discovery and the prototype are for.
