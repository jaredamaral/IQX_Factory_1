# Instruction Set: "The IQX Factory" Executive Deck

> Hand this to the Verndale-branded PowerPoint generation agent. It is an
> instruction set, not the slides themselves. Purpose: an **informative**
> executive briefing that clearly conveys the benefits without shying away from
> them — but stays out of technical/process detail.

## Objective
Create a Verndale-branded PowerPoint that explains the **IQX Factory** to a
senior executive audience. They must walk away understanding *what it is, what it
produces, and why it matters* — with the benefits made clear, and no internal
mechanics.

## Hard constraints
- **Length: 4 slides maximum** (may compress to 3 — see optional-merge note).
- **Audience: executives.** Outcome-oriented, not how-it-works.
- **Density: rich but breathable.** Per slide: one headline, one core message,
  3–4 short supporting points max, one strong visual. No paragraphs.
- **No duplication.** Each slide has a distinct job; never repeat a point.
- **Plain language.** No jargon, no internal mechanics (no mention of agents,
  pipelines, phases, files, prompts). "AI-driven" is fine as a concept.
- **Tone:** informative and confident. State benefits plainly; do not turn it
  into a hard sales pitch or an investment ask.
- **Branding:** apply Verndale visual identity, fonts, and color system per the
  configured template.
- **No invented numbers.** Use the qualitative benefit language provided. Only
  insert a metric where a slot is explicitly marked, and only if a real figure
  is supplied.

## North-star sentence (anchor everything to this)
> The IQX Factory is Verndale's repeatable, AI-driven system for turning our
> customer-intelligence playbook into a complete, market-ready offering for any
> industry — fast, consistent, and credible.

## Context the agent should know (do NOT put all of this on slides)
- Verndale's Data & Analytics practice builds **IQX Offerings**: industry-specific
  customer intelligence and activation products (e.g., **BankIQX** for mid-market
  banking). More are coming.
- The **IQX Factory** is the system that produces these offerings repeatably
  instead of hand-building each from scratch.
- Each offering it produces includes: market/industry research, a defined set of
  use cases, a customer-data model, go-to-market and sales materials, and a
  working, demonstrable prototype.
- It is **governed**: humans approve at key checkpoints, and claims are
  evidence-backed — so output is credible in front of clients.
- Built on Verndale's canonical technologies (Sigma + Snowflake); mention only as
  a one-line credibility signal, not a topic.

## Slide 1 — The Big Idea
- **Headline:** The IQX Factory
- **Sub-headline:** From one-off projects to a repeatable offering engine
- **Core message:** Verndale can now produce industry-specific customer-
  intelligence offerings the way a factory produces products — repeatably and at
  quality.
- **Supporting points (choose 3):**
  - We package our best customer-intelligence thinking into a reusable system.
  - Point it at an industry; it produces a complete, sellable offering.
  - BankIQX proved the model; the Factory lets us repeat it for any market.
- **Visual concept:** A clean "input (industry) → engine → output (offering)"
  flow, or a subtle assembly-line motif. One strong graphic.

## Slide 2 — What It Produces
- **Headline:** One system, a complete offering every time
- **Core message:** Each run yields everything needed to take an offering to
  market — not a document, but a ready package.
- **Supporting points (4 clean tiles/icons, one line each):**
  - **Market insight** — industry research and where we win
  - **The offering** — defined use cases and the customer-data foundation
  - **Go-to-market** — positioning, packaging, and sales enablement
  - **Proof** — a working, demonstrable prototype for client conversations
- **Visual concept:** 4-tile row or 2×2 grid with icons. Terse labels only.
- **Footer credibility line (small):** Built on Verndale's canonical Sigma +
  Snowflake foundation.

## Slide 3 — Why It Matters
- **Headline:** Faster, consistent, and built to scale
- **Core message:** The Factory turns offering creation into a compounding,
  low-risk advantage for Verndale.
- **Supporting points (4 benefits, each benefit + one-line substantiation):**
  - **Speed to market** — new offerings stand up in a fraction of the usual time
    *(optional metric slot: insert a real figure only if available)*
  - **Consistent quality** — the same rigor and standards applied every time
  - **Scales across industries** — one engine, many markets
  - **Low risk & credible** — human-approved at key steps and evidence-backed
- **Visual concept:** A few bold benefit callouts, or a simple before/after.
  If no metric is supplied, keep it purely qualitative — do not fabricate stats.

## Slide 4 (optional) — The Vision
- **Headline:** A growing family of IQX offerings
- **Core message:** BankIQX today; a portfolio across industries tomorrow.
- **Supporting points (3):**
  - Today: BankIQX in market.
  - Next: additional industries spun up on the same engine.
  - Each new offering gets faster and cheaper than the last.
- **Visual concept:** A portfolio/roadmap band — one filled tile (BankIQX) and
  several "next" tiles. Optional closing line.

## Optional merge (to land at 3 slides)
Fold Slide 4's vision into the bottom of Slide 3 as a single "What's next" line
plus the portfolio visual. Keep Slides 1 and 2 intact.

## Global do / don't
- **Do:** lead with outcomes; use strong visuals; keep every line scannable in
  under 3 seconds; make the benefits clear and confident.
- **Don't:** explain how it works, list internal components, repeat a message
  across slides, or invent metrics.
- **Don't:** overcrowd — if a slide needs a paragraph, cut it.
